The objective of this laboratory was to design, build, and test an optical transmitter and receiver that will integrate into the ECEn 390 Laser Tag project. 

We started out with building a schematic using Spice to simulate our reciever board. The board would filter out other waveforms such as sunlight or the lights in the room and would look for only the frequencies of other laser tag players.

After figuring out the right schematic layout, we built the physical system on a breadboard and tested our resistor and capacitor values with a laser tuned to the one we needed.
